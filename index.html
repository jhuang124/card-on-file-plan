    Everyone Else: The Existing Experience

    The card on file appears only when all of the following hold. Otherwise checkout looks exactly as it does today, and the server refuses the option even if a request asks for it.

    • Signed in, with a Zuora account (subscribers from before the Zuora migration are not offered their card)
    • A card on file that is not expired and passed its security-code check (PayPal defaults are not offered)
    • Not a trial offer, since lapsed customers cannot start another trial
    • Not already blocked from buying a second subscription: active subscribers, trial users, shared-subscription members, print subscribers and institutional users
    • The feature flag is on, on both the backend and the frontend

    05 · Billing Model

    Card Used for Each Subscription

    Each customer has one default card, and it pays for their own subscription and every gift that renews. This plan stops a gift from accidentally replacing that card. It does not let gifts live on different cards.

    Jane pays for herself with a Visa and wants Sam's gift on an Amex and Ana's gift on a Mastercard. Compare what each configuration does with that.

    Tile color shows the card each subscription renews on. A line means it renews on the account default.

    Why We Show One Card, Not Stripe's Saved-Card ListDesign rationale

    Stripe can display every card a customer has saved. At The Atlantic, Stripe stores the cards but Zuora decides which one is charged, and old cards are never removed from Stripe.

    Stripe's saved-card list (not used)

    AMEXAmex •••• 1005Replaced
    VISAVisa •••• 1881Replaced
    Picking a replaced card would either silently make it the renewal card or charge a different card than the one picked.
    This plan
    VISAPay with Visa •••• 4242
    Use a different payment method
    The card we show is the card we charge, and it is the card that renews.

    Stripe's list would also require a Stripe library upgrade, changes to the allowlist that limits which Stripe features we call, and a data backfill, because Stripe only redisplays cards marked for redisplay.

    06 · Risk

    Gift Safeguards

    Skipping card entry means someone inside a customer's account could send gifts on that customer's card. A daily limit caps the damage and an owner email surfaces it quickly; a recent sign-in requirement would stop a stolen session outright.

    Safeguards: Recommended: A + B

    Today this path stops at step 3, because gift checkout requires typing a card. Stripe's fraud screening is unlikely to catch it, since Zuora's servers place the charge and Stripe never sees the buyer's device or location.

    All Five Options ComparedA to E
    OptionProtectionCustomer FrictionBuild Cost
    A. Daily limit. After 3 card-on-file gifts in 24 hours, the donor must enter a cardLimits damage; does not prevent itNone for typical donorsSmall
    B. Owner email. "You sent a gift with Visa •••• 4242. Not you?"Detection before the gift is redeemedNoneSmall, if an existing template fits
    C. Recent sign-in. Card-on-file gifts require a sign-in within the last N minutesStrongest; a stolen session alone is not enoughAdds a sign-in step to the flow being simplifiedMedium
    D. Re-enter the security codeModerateLow; a familiar patternPoor fit: Stripe only checks a re-entered code on charges run from the browser, and Zuora runs ours
    E. Prior recipients onlyStrongExcludes most first-time giftsSmall

    Recommendation. Ship A and B in the prototype and keep C ready if Security requests it or fraud appears. The rate of account takeover on Atlantic logins is TK from Security and Customer Care; a high rate would justify shipping C from the start.

    07 · Boundaries

    Known Limitations

    Four cases fall outside the prototype. Each one falls back to today's behavior.

    Pre-Zuora Subscribers

    Lapsed subscribers from before the Zuora migration are not offered their card, because it is not linked to a Zuora account.

    Cards Requiring Bank Verification

    Cards that require verification on every charge fail, and the customer is asked for a new card.

    Gifts Paid With a New Card

    A new card still replaces the donor's own renewal card, as it does today. The one-time charge option in the Engineering Plan would change this.

    PayPal

    Not offered in the prototype. The Engineering Plan appendix sizes it.

    08 · Launch

    Rollout and Verification

    The feature sits behind flags on the backend and the frontend: off in production, on for beta. Verification runs on a beta pair with Stripe test cards and Zuora's test environment.

    • Buy a subscription, let it lapse, and confirm the account page shows the card.
    • Re-subscribe with the card on file. Confirm the subscription is active, paid by the same card, and that no new card was created.
    • Change the card in another tab, then submit the original checkout. The purchase is refused and the card refreshed.
    • As an active subscriber, buy two gifts to different recipients in one session. Both succeed and the owner email arrives. Continue until the daily limit refuses the card-on-file option.
    • Store a card that requires bank verification on every charge and re-subscribe. Expect a clear error, then success with a new card.
    • Turn the flags off and confirm everything behaves as it does today.

    09 · Later

    Future Options

    Two extensions are sized but out of scope: charging a specific card for one order, and PayPal on file.

    One-Time Charge on a Specific CardNot planned

    "One card per customer" reflects how we use Zuora, not a Zuora limit. An order can charge a specific payment method through processing_options.payment_method_id, which would let a donor pay for a gift with a different card without replacing their own renewal card. Renewals still charge the default, because our integration has no per-subscription card. Zuora may offer per-subscription cards as an add-on; that is unconfirmed.

    PayPal on FileOut of scope; sized
    CaseConfidenceBasis
    Re-subscribe with a stored agreementHighRuns in production: a PayPal purchase makes the agreement the default (purchases/serializers.py:328-345), then places the same collect_payment order (order.py:392-396) with no PayPal-specific options.
    Gift with a stored agreementMediumNever done: gift purchase always swaps in a card first (gifts/serializers.py:385-395). Order settings do not depend on the gateway (order.py:473-491), but the donor pays while the recipient owns the subscription, and it can be future-dated.
    Knowing the agreement is alivePartialPayPalBillingAgreement.cancelled_at is set on cancellation (subscriptions/fsm.py:237), but only for agreements created since that table existed.

    Scope

    • Offer PayPal on file only when a local agreement record exists and cancelled_at is empty. A dead agreement fails safely; it cannot charge the wrong account.
    • Return the PayPal email (paypal_cp.email, account.py:497); generalize reusable_card into reusable_payment_method; all guards carry over.
    • Effort: backend ~2 to 3 days, frontend ~2 days, QA ~1 to 2 days.
    • Spike first: re-subscribe on a stored agreement, a gift-shaped order on a PayPal default, and a cancelled agreement failing cleanly. Data check: are any gift invoices already paid through PayPal? Terms check: agreements are described as "Subscription to The Atlantic" (lib/paypal/client.py:56).
